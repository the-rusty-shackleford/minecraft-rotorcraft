# D-0004 — A broken aircraft can be set down and mended

Rusty (2026-10-07): a friend's Huey broke and he could not repair it, "probably because it's fully
broken and won't let him place it, but he needs to place it to repair it. If that's the case, then
for vehicles in general you should be able to place it but it's not driveable until repaired."

It was the case, and it was ours. A Vanilla Wheels car already behaves as Rusty asks: worn to
nothing it is packed into a broken item; set down again it stays, cannot drive, and the click that
would seat a player repairs it a step. An aircraft worn to nothing in the air comes down first
(D-0001) and is packed once it stands; that was done every tick for any aircraft standing at no
condition, so a broken aircraft set down from its item was packed again on its first tick, before
anyone could click it. Neither the hand nor the Mechanic Lift could reach it.

**Decision**: only a wreck made in the air is packed when it comes down (`Aircraft.wreckComingDown`,
set when the destroy is deferred, saved with the aircraft). A broken aircraft set down from its item
stays where it is put, its engine dead (a wreck's), until it is repaired by hand or on the lift; hurt
again it is packed again, as a car is. Submersibles' wreck at depth had the same rule and takes the
same fix (its D-0004).

**Why**: a broken vehicle that cannot be set down cannot be repaired at all.

**Verification**: the GameTest `aBrokenAircraftSetDownFromItsItemStaysUnflyableUntilItIsRepaired`
(eighty ticks on it is still there, asked to climb it has not, and a click repairs it a step) fails
on the old rule ("still where it was put"); `wornToNothingInTheAirItComesDownBeforeItIsAWreck...`
still packs a wreck made in the air once it is down.

**Links**: `Aircraft.destroy`, `Aircraft.tick`; D-0001; Vanilla Wheels' `Vehicle.hurt` and hand repair
(its D-0025).
