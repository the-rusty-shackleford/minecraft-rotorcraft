# D-0003 — The crop sprayer

Rusty: "some kind of dispersal attachment (optional) that helicopters can use to disperse bone
meal over large farm plots". One bone meal per crop dosed, as by hand (his call).

**Decision**:

- **The item.** `rotorcraft:crop_sprayer`. Right-click an aircraft whose profile has a `sprayer`
  mount to fit it; crouch empty-handed on the boom to take it off, the rig and its bone meal
  handed back through Carried. The boom (the mount's part) is drawn only while one is fitted, and
  packing the aircraft keeps it and its bone meal.
- **The tank.** Right-click with bone meal, or bone blocks at nine each, up to 256. The HUD shows
  it beside the hotbar.
- **Spraying.** The pilot's key switches it.
  - Each tick, in the air with the engine running, every column the boom swept since the last
    tick (`domain/Swath`; one line a tick would leave stripes at a Huey's 1.4 b/t) is looked down,
    up to 12 blocks.
  - The first block that is not air, if `#rotorcraft:sprayable` (default `#minecraft:crops`) and
    the pilot may build there, gets `BoneMealItem.applyBonemeal`: NeoForge's `BonemealEvent`, so a
    claim mod may refuse; vanilla's roll; one bone meal.
  - A column is dosed once per 40 ticks, at most 32 doses a tick.
  - An empty tank switches it off. A creative pilot sprays from an empty tank and spends nothing.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Every block under the boom*: grass and flowers would sprout over the border.
- *A cheaper dose*: Rusty chose parity with bone meal by hand.

**Verification**:
- JUnit: `SwathTest`.
- `SprayGameTests` (8): a pass at 1.4 b/t doses exactly the 8 by 16 crops under the boom at one
  bone meal each, nothing beside it, no grass; a hover doses its row once, then again after the
  cooldown; nothing from 20 up; a cancelled bone meal event spends nothing; an empty tank switches
  off; packing keeps the sprayer; a creative pilot sprays for free; fitted and taken off by hand,
  70 bone meal handed back.
- Each rule was run against its mutation and caught it: a line a tick, no cooldown, no tag, a
  creative pilot spending.
